Version history

0.1.0

Initial release of the Rust SDK, aligned with Nomba's published OpenAPI spec and the nomba-python SDK for full feature parity.

  • 99 endpoints from Nomba's OpenAPI spec, across 15 resource groups.
  • Auth via client-credentials /v1/auth/token/issue, with transparent token caching and revoke_access_token on /v1/auth/token/revoke.
  • Full Accounts surface: list/create sub accounts, parent & sub balances, suspend/reactivate, access control, and terminal assignment.
  • Virtual Accounts on /v1/accounts/virtual/*: create (parent or sub), fetch, filter, update, and expire.
  • Online Checkout covering orders, transaction management, refunds, card-detail/OTP flows, flash account numbers, and the saved-card OTP flow.
  • Charge sequence on /v1/charge/*: tokenized cards, bank lookup, card details, OTP, resend, and confirm.
  • Transfers: bank/wallet transfers from parent or sub accounts, authorize, exchange authorize, convert, and live exchange rates (/v1/transfers/*).
  • Terminals: assign/unassign (parent or sub account) and send a payment request to a terminal.
  • Airtime & Data vending with product_id and network-aware data-plan lookup on /v1/bill/*.
  • CableTV lookup, subscribe, and product-plan fetch on /v1/bill/cableTvProduct.
  • Electricity and Betting vending with provider/customer lookups.
  • Direct Debits mandates on /v1/direct-debits/*: create, list, status, update, and debit.
  • Global Collections and Global Payout (accounts, transfer/exchange authorize, inflows).
  • Sync-first design with a parallel async client (Nomba vs AsyncNomba) behind blocking / async features.
  • Typed responses via serde, with cargo install and cargo add support.
  • Cursor pagination helpers (paginate / apaginate generators).
  • Guided card-payment OTP/3DS flow (CardPaymentFlow) with sync and async variants.
  • Webhook signature verification with replay-window protection.
  • Retry-with-backoff on 429/5xx and bounded-concurrency helpers (gather_limited / gather_limited_async).
  • Optional nested request-body validation against the bundled OpenAPI spec (validation feature).
